# Question Bank Attributions

Per the M5 rule (`milestones/m5.md`): every source whose questions land in
`data/questions/` is recorded here with its license before those questions
ship.

## 3600-Plus Review Questions for Anatomy & Physiology, Volume 1

- **Author:** R. Michael Anson
- **Edition:** 5th ed.
- **License:** [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- **Source:** <https://digital.avicennamch.com/updata/services/file_file/542_20190401161908.pdf>
- **Imported:** 2026-09-01 — 1,595 questions across all 14 topics
  (`anatomical_language` through `endocrine_system`); the entire bank is
  adapted from this source.
- **Changes made:** original short-answer / fill-in items converted to
  multiple choice (distractor options written for this project); some items
  lightly reworded; questions reorganized under this project's topic and
  subtopic scheme. Original wording, including typos, otherwise preserved.
- **Share-alike:** because the source is CC BY-SA 3.0, the adapted question
  data in the affected `*.json` files is likewise available under
  CC BY-SA 3.0 if distributed.

## 3600-Plus Review Questions for Anatomy & Physiology, Volume 2

- **Author:** R. Michael Anson
- **Edition:** 3rd ed. (Dec. 2008)
- **License:** [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- **Source:** the freely redistributed PDF scan kept in `question_sources/`
  (`3600+ Review Questions Volume2 - PDFCOFFEE.COM.pdf`), circulated under
  the book's CC BY-SA license.
- **Imported:** 2026-09-09 — 656 questions across `blood`, `heart`,
  `blood_vessels`, and `lymphatic_and_immune_system` (ids `bio-anat-15-*`
  through `bio-anat-18-*`). Further topics (respiratory onward) will be added
  as they are vetted.
- **Changes made:** same treatment as Volume 1 — short-answer / fill-in items
  converted to multiple choice with project-written distractors; a few items
  lightly reworded (e.g. one grammar fix); questions reorganized under this
  project's topic and subtopic scheme. Original wording otherwise preserved.
- **Share-alike:** as with Volume 1, the adapted question data in the
  affected `*.json` files is available under CC BY-SA 3.0 if distributed.
